 Basic Demos:
  - add_frankas.py - Franka robot arm simulation
  - simulate_robot.py - Robot control examples
  - visual_materials.py - Material and rendering demos

  Robot Examples:
  - UR10e robot manipulation (source/standalone_examples/api/isaacsim.robot.manipulators/ur10e/)
  - Humanoid and quadruped robots
  - Pick and place tasks



 To run any demo:
  cd _build/windows-x86_64/release
  ./python.bat ../../../source/standalone_examples/api/[category]/[demo_name].py
